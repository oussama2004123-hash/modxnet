Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information about you.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information modXnet.com ("ModXnet", "we", "us") collects when you use the website at https://modxnet.com, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to the website and to messages you send us through it.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Accounts are created by signing in with Google. We receive your name, email address, Google account ID and profile picture link from Google. You can change your display name and avatar in your profile.
  • Reviews and comments. When you post a rating, review or comment, we store the text, the rating, the game it relates to and the date, linked to your account.
  • Messages. When you use our contact form or copyright form, we store your name, email address, subject, message and any other details you enter (for a copyright notice, for example, the work concerned, the links reported and your signature).

Information collected automatically

  • Download and offer sessions. When you open the download window on a game page, our server creates a random session code and records it with the game, the time and your IP address. To show offers that are available in your country and on your device, our server sends your IP address and your browser's user-agent string to AdBlueMedia. If you complete an offer, AdBlueMedia tells our server which offer was completed and the commission paid, and we store that with the session.
  • Usage and device information. Through Google Analytics we collect information about how the website is used, such as pages viewed, buttons clicked (for example download buttons and offers), the game and platform involved, the referring website, approximate location (city or country), device type, operating system and browser.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our web server and our network provider (Cloudflare) process your IP address, the pages you request, your browser type and the time of the request to deliver the website and protect it against abuse.
  • Data stored in your browser. We use a login cookie and a few local-storage entries, described in our Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive information such as health data.

2. How we use information

  • To run the website, keep you signed in and display your reviews and comments.
  • To show offers that fit your country and device, confirm completed offers and unlock downloads.
  • To answer your messages and handle privacy and copyright requests.
  • To send emails about your account, such as a welcome message when you sign in. We may also send occasional announcements about the website to registered users; you can ask us to stop at any time through our contact form.
  • To measure and improve the website and understand which features are used.
  • To show advertising that helps pay for the website (see section 4).
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, abuse and security problems, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your name or email address.

3. Third-party services

We share information only as needed with the services that help us run ModXnet:

  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): used to create and sign in to accounts.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager (Google LLC): website measurement. We have configured Google Analytics not to use Google signals and to remove email addresses and certain personal fields from collected page addresses. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Google AdSense (Google LLC): advertising, see section 4.
  • AdBlueMedia: supplies the offers shown in the download window and confirms completed offers. When you click an offer you leave ModXnet and the advertiser's or AdBlueMedia's own terms and privacy policy apply to anything you do there, including any information you give them.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.): content delivery and security for all traffic to the website.
  • Email delivery (Google Gmail): used to send account emails and to forward copies of contact messages to our inbox.
  • DeepSeek: when a contact message is written in a language other than English, its text may be sent to DeepSeek's AI translation service to produce an English copy for our team.
  • Content providers: fonts, icons and some images and avatars are loaded from Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare), DiceBear and image-hosting services, which receive your IP address when your browser loads them.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of ModXnet, our users or others, or as part of a transfer of the website to a new owner.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

Some pages on ModXnet show ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.

More information is available in Google's advertising policy.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies and browser storage for sign-in, measurement and advertising. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google Analytics cookies are switched off by default. Full details, including how to control cookies, are in our Cookie Policy.

6. Public content

Your display name, avatar, ratings, reviews and comments are shown publicly on game pages. Do not include personal information in reviews or comments that you do not want others to see.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account information, reviews and comments: until you ask us to delete your account or the content, or we remove it.
  • Download sessions that do not lead to a completed offer: deleted automatically after 7 days. Sessions with a completed offer are kept as a record of the commission paid.
  • Messages: for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the request.
  • Google Analytics data: event-level data is kept for 2 months under our current Google Analytics settings; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Web server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent. You can:

  • Send a request through our contact form and choose "Privacy request". Please use the email address linked to your account so we can identify it. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.
  • Edit your display name and avatar in your profile at any time.
  • Control cookies through your browser and the options listed in our Cookie Policy.
  • Remove ModXnet's access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account settings.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Performance of a contract: providing your account, reviews and the download feature you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests: running and securing the website, preventing fraud, handling messages and copyright notices, and showing offers relevant to your country and device.
  • Consent: analytics and advertising cookies where consent is required by law.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

10. Notice for US residents

We do not sell personal information for money. However, allowing Google and other ad partners to use cookies for personalized advertising may be considered "selling" or "sharing" personal information under some US state laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. You can opt out of personalized advertising using the options in section 4 and by enabling the Global Privacy Control signal in a browser that supports it. You may also exercise the rights described in section 8. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11. Children

ModXnet is not directed to children under 13, and users in the EEA and UK must be at least 16 or have permission from a parent or guardian. Many games listed on ModXnet carry age ratings for older players. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security and international transfers

We use HTTPS encryption, restricted administrator access and the security features of our hosting and network providers to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Our servers and the third-party services listed above may be located in countries other than yours, including the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. For any question about this policy or your data, please use our contact form.

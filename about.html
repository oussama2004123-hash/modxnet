About ModXnet

What this website is and how it works.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

modXnet.com ("ModXnet", "we", "us") is a website about games for mobile devices and PC. We publish game pages with descriptions, screenshots, ratings and reviews, and we provide access to downloads through a short sponsored step.

What you can do on ModXnet

  • Browse game pages by category or search for a title.
  • Read descriptions, screenshots and system information for each listing.
  • Sign in with Google to post ratings, reviews and comments.
  • Use the download button on a game page. Before a download starts, you are asked to complete one offer from our advertising partner (for example installing and opening an app, or answering a short survey).
  • Use the help chat for answers to common download questions.

How ModXnet is funded

ModXnet is free to use. We earn money in two ways:

  • Sponsored offers. Offers shown in the download window are supplied by our advertising partner AdBlueMedia and its advertisers. We receive a commission when an offer is completed. Completing an offer is your choice, and each offer has its own terms set by the advertiser.
  • Advertising. Some pages show ads served by Google AdSense.

More details are in our Disclaimer, Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Independence from game publishers

ModXnet is an independent website. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game developer, publisher or platform. Game titles, names, logos, artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the games being discussed. A game appearing on ModXnet does not mean its publisher has released, approved or licensed that version.

Respecting rights holders

If you own the rights to material that appears on ModXnet and you believe it is used without permission, please follow our DMCA / Copyright Policy. We review every notice we receive.

Contact

Questions, feedback or problems with the website can be sent through our contact form.

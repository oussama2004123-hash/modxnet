Cookie Policy

Which cookies and browser storage we use, and how to control them.

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar feature that lets a website remember settings on your device. This Cookie Policy explains which of these modXnet.com ("ModXnet") and its partners use. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

Strictly necessary

These are needed for the website to work and are always active.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the website is used. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, these cookies are switched off by default, and Google Analytics receives only cookieless measurement signals.

Advertising

Some pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may set cookies, such as __gads, __gpi and __eoi on modxnet.com, and cookies on Google domains such as doubleclick.net, to serve ads, limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and, where permitted, personalise ads based on your visits to this and other websites. The exact cookies are controlled by Google; see how Google uses cookies.

Other third parties

When you click an offer in the download window you are taken to AdBlueMedia and advertiser websites, which may set their own cookies under their own policies. Fonts, icons and some images are loaded from Google Fonts, cdnjs, DiceBear and image-hosting services, which may receive standard technical information from your browser.

How to control cookies

  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser's settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop sign-in from working.
  • Personalised ads: use Google's Ads Settings, www.aboutads.info or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Google Analytics: install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Global Privacy Control: you can enable it in a supporting browser to signal that you opt out of personalised advertising.

Changes and contact

We will update this Cookie Policy when the cookies we use change. Questions can be sent through our contact form.
